📱 Children & Screen Time

Awareness content about the risks of electronic devices on children, designed for infographic and presentation use.

🔹 Overview

This project provides simplified and structured content about the impact of excessive screen usage on children, covering health, psychological, social, and behavioral aspects.

📚 Key Effects

🩺 Health

  • Eye strain
  • Poor sleep
  • Neck & back pain

🧠 Psychological

  • Low attention span
  • Anxiety
  • Screen addiction

👥 Social

  • Isolation
  • Reduced interaction

⚡ Behavioral

  • Irritability
  • Aggressive behavior

💡 Tips

  • Limit screen time
  • Encourage physical activity
  • No screens before sleep
  • Monitor content
  • Increase family interaction
